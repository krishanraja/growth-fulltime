# Browser access: read with Krish's logins, never act

Krish may run this engine with a browser signed in to Full Time's accounts.
That lets a job read what only he can see: PostHog, Google Analytics,
TikTok Studio, YouTube Studio, Instagram's insights and the product inbox.
The browser is for **reading only**.

Prefer approved read-only Control Center aggregate records when they already
carry the needed measure. Keep source dates and observation windows. Use the
browser for gaps those records cannot answer. No browser does not mean no
data: report verified backend aggregates and mark only the unread measures
unavailable.

Krish's 17 October execution limits exclude his personal social accounts
entirely. Verify Full Time's brand identity before reading a signed-in social
dashboard. Do not inspect personal profiles, DMs, followers or groups to
source growth work. If only a personal identity is available, skip the
account. Public audience research does not authorise use of his login.
Cold outbound email and personal-account drafts are excluded too; see
`EXECUTION-PLAN.md`.

## The line you never cross

In a signed-in browser you may open pages, scroll, search, sort and filter,
open analytics and reports, and copy numbers out. You may **never**:

- type into a post, caption, comment, reply, message, email, DM or chat box,
  not even to "prepare" a draft there (drafts live in the pack);
- press Post, Publish, Upload, Share, Send, Reply, Like, Upvote, Follow,
  Join, Boost, Promote, Schedule, Save, Install, Confirm, Approve, Archive,
  Delete or Pay;
- launch, edit or fund an ad, a boost, a Spark Ad or a creator payment;
- change a PostHog project, an insight, a cohort, a feature flag or a
  setting; change anything in Vercel, Supabase or Google Analytics;
- change a profile, a bio, a link, a channel or an account setting;
- accept or decline anything (invitations, terms, cookies beyond what is
  needed to read), or sign in to an account Krish is not already signed in
  to.

If a page shows a pop-up that needs one of these, close it or leave the page.
If a task seems to need one, write it as a draft in the pack, with exactly
where to click, for Krish to do.

If an account shows you signed out, don't sign in. Say "signed out of
<place>" in the pack's headline and carry on with what is public.

## Listeners' details stay where they are

PostHog's persons, the product inbox and DMs show real people: emails,
names, what they wrote. Read **insights and counts**, not people. If you
open a person or an email to check a number, copy nothing from it except
counts and themes. Nothing that identifies a listener goes into a pack or a
file (AGENTS.md, rule 8).
