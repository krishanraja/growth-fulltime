# Full Time growth operating system

Read AGENTS.md and EXECUTION-PLAN.md first. Krish's current constraints override
the historical channel banks in knowledge/. That source is research history,
not an active calendar or permission to recruit through personal accounts.

## Outcome

Grow completed, returning listens and voluntary recommendations. The first
learning cohort is thirty distinct consenting testers who finish a show.
The older ambition is 1,000 reachable completers by 8 November 2026; it is not
a forecast. A page view, play, form submission or follower is not that user.

Full Time's December bar and product canon govern public release. Measure its
whole bar, including supply reliability, factual incidents, consent, mature
seven-day return, actual second-AI-Pundit playback and the disappointment
survey with denominators. Never relax the thresholds to make a report green.

## Work order

1. Read live product truth and rail. If no match has four of six AI Pundits,
   hold promotion and hand off the stored-verdict diagnosis to full-time.
2. Establish the consenting source of testers and the event/identity contract.
   If either is unavailable, name that dependency and keep measures unknown.
3. Verify first listen, completion, actual same-match comparison, exact-edition
   sharing and an optional permitted way back. Product changes are briefs.
4. Prepare reusable artwork/audio locally. Quotations come from published
   transcripts and need timing review. A renderer preview is not publication.
5. Start one branded YouTube test only after release and channel gates pass.
   Use a real mobile path from the surface; Shorts links are not assumed
   clickable. Human publication and its actual URL are recorded separately.
6. Review a complete observation window before expanding formats or channels.

## Channel policy

Allowed work uses the website/feed, verified Full Time brand identities,
voluntary listener sharing and inbound or explicitly opted-in product email.
Personal social accounts, founder launches, personal WhatsApp groups, cold
email, press/creator pitches, paid promotion and FPL activation are outside
this execution cycle. Do not generate held drafts for excluded work.

Email permission must cover its purpose. A launch-note subscription does not
permit a recap. Private recipient data stays in the original system. The only
address in Full Time copy is krish@fulltime.fm.

## Creative

One concept per eligible match morning. Start with two genuinely different
arguments about the same real match. Use covers, the match seal and approved
source audio, with AI disclosure and one call to action. Do not invent a line
for an AI Pundit. A one-edition preview never pretends to be a comparison.

Creative IDs follow:
`YT_CLASH_SHORT_C01_H01_V1_20261017`.
Use a new version for a changed public creative; keep the concept number for
related hooks. Every destination is built by `growth.py link` and names the
actual drop and published AI Pundit. Initial assets support YouTube and private
site previews. No new image, voice or video generation is needed.

## Measurement and experiments

`MEASUREMENT.md` owns definitions and aggregate query requirements. Platform
attention explains discovery; it never substitutes for completed listens.
The engine stores aggregates only. Whole-window distinct users are queried
upstream, never reconstructed by adding daily or rolling counts.

Each experiment declares its audience, hypothesis, one changed variable,
primary outcome, guardrails, minimum sample, duration and stopping rule before
it runs. Product tests need at least seven days. Underpowered or immature
results are inconclusive; they cannot justify scale. Keep negative results.
A pause, a switch-intent event, a share attempt and a new attributed completer
are different observations. Private forwarding and true viral coefficient
remain unknown without a valid observation method.

Twenty-four and seventy-two-hour creative readings support triage only.
A week with no available follow-up show needs a supply annotation beside the
retention cohort. Do not diagnose audience disinterest from absent supply.

## Daily and weekly operation

The morning batch syncs once, reads imported approved aggregates, evaluates
the current constraint and prepares at most three actions. HOLD runs do not
regenerate promotional banks. The weekly run reads the same durable history,
uses a whole seven-day window and prepares native weekly-review findings,
kill_list and double_down without touching human decision fields.

Control Centre receives a handoff contract, not writes from this session.
Its implementation must use existing Growth, Board and Home surfaces. Packs
and outbox files are private checkpoints until a separate implementation
verifies a complete product-isolated round trip.

Target zero incremental marketing cash. Record observed model/audio calls,
external spend and human minutes separately; missing usage is not zero.
