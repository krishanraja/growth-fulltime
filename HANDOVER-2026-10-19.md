# Handover, 19 October 2026: Control Center protocol adopted

Read AGENTS.md, current source state, PLAYBOOK.md and EXECUTION-PLAN.md. This
adds to [HANDOVER-2026-10-17.md](HANDOVER-2026-10-17.md); everything there
still holds except that run reports now go to Control Center through its
growth engine protocol ([brief](docs/CONTROL-CENTER-BRIEF.md)).

## Delivered

- `engine/control_center.py`: the one Control Center transport. HTTPS only, no
  redirects, 20 second timeouts, bounded responses, the bearer read at run
  time and never printed. It journals attempts, pulls the directive, reports
  with `Idempotency-Key`, reads the digest back, reads decisions and journals
  one terminal state with a lower_snake code.
- `engine/reporting.py`: the canonical digest (it reproduces the worked
  example), a strict schema check that refuses unknown fields at every depth,
  and the mapping from the engine's run, history and rulings to the report.
- `python3 growth.py cc morning|weekly|dispatch`, the automations ending with
  it, and `.github/workflows/control-center.yml` on `cc-growth-directive` and
  manual dispatch.
