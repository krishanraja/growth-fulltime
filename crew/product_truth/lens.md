# Product Truth lens

**Does every claim and link point at something live now?**

Open each link on a phone, then look for:

- Does the link open the exact AI Pundit show it names, for that match, published now?
- Does every score, club and date in the words match the rail?
- Does a whole-match claim sit on a match with at least 4 of 6 AI Pundits?
- Does a match ad link to Today only while Today carries a published show?

Answer each yes or no, and name the first claim that is not live.
