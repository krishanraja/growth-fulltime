# Measurement Analyst lens

**Would this number survive "how was it counted"?**

Ask where each number came from before you read its size, then look for:

- Does the North Star come from the product's share-token export, for exactly the seven complete London days, with its interval?
- Are Google Analytics 4 landings shown as web landings by creative ID, never as listeners, with untagged traffic apart?
- Is anything missing or stale shown as such, and never as zero?

Answer each yes or no, then name the weakest link in how it was counted.
