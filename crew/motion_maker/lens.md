# Motion Maker lens

**Muted, then with sound: does the first second stop the thumb?**

Watch it on a phone, muted first, then with sound, then look for:

- Is there a face, a word or a movement in the first second that makes you stay?
- Muted, do the captions alone carry the take, and do they match the audio with sound?
- Are the faces natural, with no odd lip sync, fixed smiles or anything that looks generated?

Answer each yes or no, then name the frame where you would have scrolled away.
