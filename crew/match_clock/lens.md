# Match Clock lens

**Is this the match fans are arguing about?**

Read the ranked fixtures as a fan would on a Sunday night, then look for:

- Is the top match the one with the rivalry, the late goal or the decision people are still angry about?
- Was each take timed from the show's publish, while the argument is still live?
- Is any take about a match whose window has already closed?

Answer each yes or no, then name the one fixture you would move up or strike.
