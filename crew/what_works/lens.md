# What-Works Analyst lens

**What would we stop doing tomorrow, and what evidence flips it?**

Read the learning report as someone who has to act on it in the morning, then look for:

- Is each "confirmed" claim backed by two matchweeks, 20 events per arm and |z| of 2 or more, with no overlapping test?
- Does each claim say the exact evidence that would flip it?
- Are listener counts the outcome, with web landings only a leading signal?

Answer each yes or no, then name the one thing to stop and the one to do more of.
