# Channel & Pack Steward lens

**Is it native to its platform, or a cross-post?**

Look at each item as a regular user of that platform would, then look for:

- Does its length, caption, hashtags and first line fit that platform, not just the one it was written for?
- Is the AI label set, and is the one tracked link where that platform lets people tap it?
- Would it look out of place beside the posts that do well there this week?

Answer each yes or no, then name the item that most reads like a cross-post.
