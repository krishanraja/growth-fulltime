# Librarian lens

**From START-HERE, can Krish find this in ten seconds?**

Start at `00_START-HERE/START-HERE.md`, time yourself, then look for:

- Does a "This week" link lead straight to the run or report, in two clicks or fewer?
- Does the file's name say its week, match, format, AI Pundit and channel at a glance?
- Is there exactly one copy, with nothing loose at the root or in a folder outside the tree?

Answer each yes or no, then name the one file that took longest to find.
