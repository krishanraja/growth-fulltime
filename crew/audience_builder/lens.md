# Audience Builder lens

**Would a fan who lands on this profile follow it within three seconds, and why?**

Look at it as a fan who has just tapped through from a shared take:

- Does the name say Full Time, and does the first bio line say "AI Pundit" and what it is?
- Is the pinned post the best take of the last 14 days?
- Is the AI visible, with no league or broadcaster marks?
- Is the one link tracked to fulltime.fm, and do the other brand profiles link here?

Answer each yes or no, then name the one change that would most raise the follow rate.
