# Take & Script Writer lens

**Would a fan send this exact line to one named mate?**

Read it once, as a fan of one of the two clubs, then look for:

- Is it one AI Pundit's real argument, quoted exactly, about a moment or a decision?
- Would you know who to send it to, and would they reply?
- Does anything need explaining, or lean on a promise, a number or a joke at a fan's expense?

Answer each yes or no, then write the one line you would send instead, if any.
