# Card Maker lens

**At thumbnail size, does a ten-year-old get it in one second?**

Shrink the card to the size it shows in a feed, then look for:

- Can you read the match, the AI Pundit and the one line without zooming?
- Is "AI Pundit" visible, with no league or broadcaster marks and no club crests?
- Is every character drawn, with no boxes, gaps or clipped words?

Answer each yes or no, then name the one thing to cut so it reads faster.
