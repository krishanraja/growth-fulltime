# Voice Lock lens

**Without the script, does each voice sound like itself and alive?**

Listen with your eyes closed, all the way through, then look for:

- Could you name each AI Pundit from the voice alone: Reporter brisk and wry, Gaffer clipped, Doomer deadpan?
- Does it sound like an argument someone means, or like a reading?
- Is the AI said out loud in the first 5 seconds?

Answer each yes or no, then name the one moment where a voice goes flat.
