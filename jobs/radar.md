# Job: radar (every day)

Kind `radar`. "Demand and the fans' words" in `PLAYBOOK.md`. Time for Krish:
about 5 minutes.

1. `python3 growth.py sync`. Use the `fulltime_sha` it prints.
2. **Comments on Full Time's own posts** (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X,
   Reddit, in the signed-in browser or in public; read only). Sort each
   useful comment into:
   - a fan arguing with an AI Pundit (the best kind: it is the product
     working);
   - a question;
   - a wrong-fact report (a score, a name, a fact);
   - an "AI slop" objection;
   - a rival fan;
   - spam.

   Draft a reply for each one worth answering, up to 10, best first:
   - `platform`: where the comment is;
   - `target_url`: the post;
   - `target_name`: the commenter's public handle;
   - `text`: the reply, in Full Time's voice, saying it is AI where it
     matters.

   A wrong-fact report is never argued with. Make it an item with
   `platform: other`, `why` "escalate: wrong fact", the post, and the claim
   as the commenter put it, with no drafted reply. It goes to a full-time
   session the same day (full-time `docs/10-support.md`: a wrong fact is
   P1). Answer an "AI slop" objection with what is true: AI-written,
   synthetic voices, checked facts, and shows that fail their checks are
   withheld.
3. **What fans are arguing about.** In public, read the morning after each
   match:
   - the post-match threads in the subreddits of the clubs that played (read
     only; never post);
   - football TikTok and YouTube Shorts search for the match;
   - FPL chatter about the gameweek;
   - AI audio news, for the launch story (`jobs/launch.md`).

   For up to 5 openings, write an item with `platform: other`:
   - `text`: the argument fans are having, where you saw it (url), the
     date, which AI Pundit's show takes a side on it (from the rail), the
     content gap, a suggested format from the PLAYBOOK bank, and your
     confidence (high, medium or low);
   - `why`: one line.

   Never claim a view count, a trend or a volume you did not read.
4. **Reddit groundwork.** For each subreddit Krish belongs to
   (`local/lists/reddit.md`), note the rules on self-promotion as written in
   its sidebar or wiki today, and whether its moderators have answered. A
   subreddit with no answer gets no link. Draft a message to the moderators
   only for the next subreddit in the list, and only once the push gate is
   OPEN (`platform: reddit`, `needs` per the rulings).
5. Headline: for example "Radar: 4 replies (1 wrong-fact escalation), 3
   openings: Forest fans think the Doomer was too kind". Check, then post.
