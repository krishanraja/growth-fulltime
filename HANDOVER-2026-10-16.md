**Recording a ruling, in order:**
1. Krish rules, in his own words.
2. A full-time session adds the decision to full-time's decision log
   (`docs/12-roadmap.md`, a heading like `### 2026-10-20 - Fan channels open`)
   and changes whatever doc the ruling changes.
3. An interactive session here sets the ruling to `approved` in
   `rulings.json`, with the date, Krish's words and that exact heading as
   `evidence`, adds a dated line to `CHANGES.md`, and runs the self-test. The
   sync then shows it in force.

**Expect a STOP when a ruling lands.** D1, D4 and D5 are likely to change
full-time sections the checker fingerprints (for example `docs/07-marketing.md`, "Release gate").
The next sync prints STOP and `state/FULLTIME-NOW.md` shows each changed
section with its new fingerprint in the "Now" column. In an interactive
session: read the new words, change the rules in `growth.py` if the words
changed what is allowed, copy the "Now" values into `RULES_BASIS`, and run
`python3 growth.py --self-test`. That is the design: no pack passes on rules
nobody has read.
