**Where it stands:** 116 entries. 80 enforced, 23 open, 4 product items, 5 owner items, 4 human judgment.

**What each status means.**

- **Enforced:** code enforces it and the named test proves it. A note may say what the test does not cover.
- **Open:** it can be checked in code, but not all of it is yet. The entry says what is missing.
- **Product item:** it belongs to full-time or Control Center, so here it is a brief.
- **Owner item:** Krish decides it or does it.
- **Human judgment:** a person's call, such as the 11/10 verdict, humour or natural faces.
