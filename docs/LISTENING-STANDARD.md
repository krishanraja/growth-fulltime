# Listening quality before growth production

Ruling (Krish, 2026-10-22): the Reporter audition B is the reference for a faster, natural and opinionated product delivery. Apply this learning to both the product and growth repositories. This is a direction for production, not a claim that an unheard full episode passed.

The product owns the canonical [spoken production and listening standard](https://github.com/krishanraja/full-time/blob/main/docs/24-listening-standard.md). Its initial implementation is a candidate pending merge and deployment. Until sync can retrieve it, retain that gap explicitly. Never claim a local branch is live product truth.

## What changes in this engine

- Choose a take a fan would argue with or send on. Formal accuracy alone does not make a clip enjoyable. Do not ask a caption to rescue dull audio.
- Reuse the exact published performance and transcript. A fast, expressive advertisement linked to a stiff product edition overpromises the first listen.
- Listen to the whole source edition before selecting excerpts. Judge its opening, natural wording, point of view, pace, emphasis and ending. Technical decode, transcript fidelity and loudness checks are necessary but do not certify enjoyment.
- Record a human listening verdict separately from technical checks. A short audition marked closer does not approve a full episode, a new persona or a promotional release.
- In private creative review evidence, name the actual source edition/audio version, exact quote, in/out times, listening verdict and unresolved holds. If listening evidence is missing, hold the creative review rather than call it ready.
- Share the persona's own take. Reporter is brisk and wry; Gaffer clipped and candid; Numbers Guy curious and animated; Romantic emotionally invested; Doomer conversational deadpan; Wind-Up gleeful and teasing. These are separate performances, not one host with changed labels.

## Boundaries that still apply

No new paid generation, video or channel publication follows from this standard. Keep release, verified brand identity, consent, exact edition links and the minimum four published AI Pundits gate. A single improved Reporter cannot be sold as a ready six-persona experience.

Locked fictional advertising dialogue and casting remain protected. The prior remote-switch concept must retain its separately approved text, characters and performance-review sequence. Product uncertainty rules do not retroactively rewrite fictional satire; satire is not evidence that the live product already performs that scene.

## Execution order

Finish and listen to the product Reporter remake first. Then audition the other five product personas on one suitable evidence pack. Approve their differences before full-length production. When enough editions genuinely pass and existing growth gates are satisfied, extract exact published clips. Compare two honest hooks and track completed listens, return listens and voluntary sharing. No invented audience proof or public quality guarantee.
